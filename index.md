## 3. What the sources establish, what I infer, what I assume
- **Established (from the postings):** all three ask for a programming language (Python), owning a problem end to end, and working directly with the people who use the work. The bank internship adds governance, privacy and compliance; the AI-company roles add building with LLMs.
- **My inference:** the same core skills carry across banking and AI companies, so a general portfolio keeps more options open than one aimed at a single employer.
- **Assumptions:** three postings from large New York-area employers are a small sample; titles alone do not show that work is new; listings close quickly.
