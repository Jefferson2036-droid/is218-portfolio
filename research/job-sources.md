## Notes per posting

### 1. FDE — Palantir Technologies (new grad)
- **Where:** Chicago, IL, hybrid, full-time; 25–50% travel. (Palantir posts the same new-grad FDE role for several cities; a New York version also exists.)
- **Pay:** about $135,000–$145,000 base, plus equity/bonus.
- **What the job actually does:** Embed with a customer's team and own a problem end to end: talk to users and executives, decide the architecture with other engineers, work with large datasets, build custom applications, and set up AI/LLM workflows for the customer's operations. The posting stresses responsibility from day one; in its words, "You will not be handed a ticket queue."
- **Required skills:** Proficiency in at least one language (Python, Java, C++, TypeScript/JavaScript or similar). Must graduate December 2026 or spring 2027.
- **Preferred skills:** A degree in an engineering or quantitative field (CS, ECE, math, software engineering, physics, data science).
- **Evidence they ask for:** Not years of experience. The application asks for your proudest project and something you're proud of that isn't on your résumé.
- **What surprised me:** The technical bar on paper is low (one language); the real test is ownership and working with customers.

### 2. AI-related technology role — OpenAI
- **Where:** New York City office, hybrid (3 days a week); occasional travel to customers.
- **Pay:** $251,000–$278,000 base plus equity.
- **What the job actually does:** Work inside customer tech companies' product and engineering teams to turn new model capabilities into shipped features: pick use cases, design the architecture, build prototypes and evaluations, fix integrations, and take systems to production. Feeds what it learns back to OpenAI's product and research teams.
- **Required skills:** Python, JavaScript or TypeScript at production quality; leading AI/ML systems from unclear start to production; current knowledge of agents, prompt and context engineering, evaluation, safety and security; strong judgment and communication with technical leaders.
- **Evidence they ask for:** 8+ years of hands-on technical experience and a track record of shipping production AI systems with measurable impact.
- **What surprised me:** It is the same customer-embedded work as an FDE, but senior. It shows where the FDE path leads after years of experience, not where it starts.

### 3. AI role in another industry — JPMorganChase (banking), summer internship
- **Where:** New York Metro, Columbus, Chicago, Delaware, Plano, Palo Alto; hybrid. (From job-board copies; to confirm on JPMorganChase's own page.)
- **Pay:** one job board lists $45.67–$55.29/hour. NOT CONFIRMED on the employer page.
- **What the job actually does:** build end-to-end data, analytics and AI/ML solutions that turn business goals into measurable outcomes; design data platforms and pipelines, develop production-ready models and dashboards, and follow data governance, privacy and compliance rules, on agile teams.
- **Eligibility:** bachelor's or master's in a quantitative or technical field; graduating December 2027 – August 2028 (fits me); no prior work experience required.
- **Tools named on the program page:** Python, SQL, AWS, Copilot, Snowflake, Databricks.
- **Related:** a "Data for Good" hackathon (posted 2026-11) is a possible pathway into the program.

- Pay ranges are what each employer posted; they don't include bonuses or equity value.
