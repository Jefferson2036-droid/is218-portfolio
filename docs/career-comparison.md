## Decision
Decided 2026-11-23 (revised the same day).

**Overall direction: forward-deployed / applied data and AI work, kept general.** The common thread across all three postings is the same kind of work: sit with the people who have a problem, build the data or AI tool that solves it, check that it works, and explain it. The portfolio presents me through that kind of work, not through one employer or industry, so my options stay open.

**The postings are research, not applications.** I am not applying to these roles now. I use them to learn what this kind of work asks for and to decide what my portfolio should prove.

**Two areas the portfolio will show (subcategories under the general direction):**
| Area | What it shows | Postings that informed it |
|---|---|---|
| Data and AI in a business setting (example industry: banking) | TD Bank work under compliance and fraud rules; Python and SQL; the Bloomberg economic analysis; a future data/dashboard project | #3 JPMorganChase Data & AI |
| Customer-facing builds | Arena Climb (owned end to end); Customer Studio (a customer's workflow, with a team); EV Charge-n-Pay (led planning and sprints) | #1 Palantir FDE, #2 OpenAI Applied AI |

**What I learned from the comparison:** banking and AI-company roles ask for the same core: Python, working with real users and stakeholders, owning a problem end to end, and handling data responsibly. Banking adds governance and compliance; AI companies add LLM workflows. My TD Bank work already covers the governance side.

**Next learning step:** a small data/AI project with public financial data and a dashboard, plus hands-on time with one cloud data tool.
